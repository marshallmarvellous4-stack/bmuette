# BMUETTE look

Two ideas combined: **clean white cards** (Pinterest-style calm, space for photos and video to lead) on **polished black** (premium, cinematic).
Content is the colour; the interface stays quiet.

| Token | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ink | `#0B0B0D` | App background |
| ink-raised | `#17181B` | Bars, sheets |
| card | `#FFFFFF` | Product and post cards, forms |
| card-text | `#111214` | Text on cards |
| text-on-ink | `#F5F5F7` | Text on black |
| muted | `#8E9097` | Secondary text |
| line | `rgba(255,255,255,0.08)` | Dividers on black |
| accent | `#FFFFFF` | Primary buttons on black; black buttons on white cards |

Rules: one accent only (white or black, whichever contrasts). Big rounded corners (20 to 28). Generous space. Photos edge to edge inside cards.
No gradients except the black "polish" on large surfaces. Motion is short (150 to 250 ms) and springy, and every tap has a haptic of matching weight.
